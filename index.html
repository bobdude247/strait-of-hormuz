Controls

  • Keyboard: WASD/Arrows pan map (hold Shift to pan faster), +/- zoom, Tab cycles escorts, click map to set escort waypoint, I/J/K/L manually steer selected escort, F damage control, 1/2/3 threat priority.
  • Tankers: Tap a tanker to select it, then tap water to shift its lane within the navigable sea corridor. Use U/O to lane-shift selected tanker.
  • Controller: Left stick camera, LB/RB cycle escorts, D-pad steers selected escort, A damage control, X sets waypoint at screen center.
  • Touch/Mouse: Drag map to scroll, mouse wheel pinch-style zoom, tap/click escort/tanker to select, tap/click water to set escort waypoint or tanker lane shift.
  • Weapons: Destroyers auto-fire layered defense: long-range SAM + close-range CIWS.
  • Traffic: Tankers run both directions (Kharg/Gulf side ↔ Arabian Sea side), with FULL/EMPTY states and toll boost gates.
  • Threat Model: Hostile launches are from the Iranian-side shoreline only; allied defense pickups can grant temporary shield intercept charges.
  • Progression: Every 3 delivered tankers grants 1 power-up point. Spend points with 7 (new escort), 8 (weapon tier), 9 (damage-control tier).
  • Traffic Jam Zones: Two anchored tanker clusters now stage before entering transit lanes, inspired by real traffic grouping behavior.
  • Navigation Rules: Tankers auto-separate in-lane; collisions and grounding now cause damage/penalties if ships leave safe water corridor.
